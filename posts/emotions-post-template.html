The Day We Let Our Hearts Breathe

By Adhira 8 min read Aug 2026

If our hearts could speak, I wonder how many names they would remember, how many moments they would hold, and how many things they would quietly ask us to let go of.

There are certain things in life that we carry for far longer than we ever intended to.

A few words someone said without thinking. A moment that changed the way we looked at a person. A disappointment we never quite knew how to explain. A memory that once meant everything to us, but slowly became something we wished we could leave behind.

We carry them quietly.

Not because we want to.
Sometimes, we simply don't know how to put them down.

And perhaps, somewhere along the way, we begin to understand that forgiveness is not about changing what happened.

It is about changing what we choose to carry from it.

We often think forgiveness means forgetting.

We imagine that forgiving someone means telling ourselves that what happened was not important, that the pain was not real, or that the person who hurt us did nothing wrong.

But I don't think forgiveness asks us to forget.

I think forgiveness asks us to remember differently.

To look at something that once made our hearts heavy and slowly reach a place where it no longer has the power to take away our peace.

Because some things cannot be undone.

Some conversations cannot be taken back.

Some moments cannot be rewritten.

And some people may never become the people we hoped they would be.

But life does not end at the places where we were disappointed.

There is still so much life waiting beyond them.

There are mornings we haven't woken up to yet, sunsets we haven't stopped to admire, songs we haven't heard at the right moment, people we haven't met, conversations that will make us laugh until our cheeks hurt, and little ordinary moments that may one day become some of our favourite memories.

Why should we let something from yesterday decide how much happiness we are allowed to feel today?

Maybe forgiveness begins when we realise that our heart deserves a future that is not constantly interrupted by its past.

Healing Doesn't Always Move in a Straight Line

It is not always easy.

There are days when we may feel ready to let go, and there are other days when an old memory suddenly returns and reminds us of everything we thought we had already healed from.

That is okay.

Healing does not always move in a straight line.

Sometimes, it is one step forward and a quiet pause.

Sometimes, it is remembering without crying.

Sometimes, it is hearing a name that once carried so much weight and realising that your heart no longer beats differently.

And sometimes, healing is simply waking up one morning and noticing that you thought about yourself before you thought about what happened to you.

That is a beautiful kind of freedom.

When Forgiveness Becomes Freedom

Because forgiveness is not always a grand moment.

It can be incredibly quiet.

It can be the moment you stop waiting for an apology.

It can be the moment you understand that you may never receive the explanation you wanted, and yet you decide that your peace cannot depend on someone else's words.

It can be the moment you stop asking, “Why did they do that to me?” and begin asking, “What can I do for myself now?”

How can I care for myself now?

And perhaps that is when the heart begins to heal.

Not because the past suddenly becomes beautiful.

But because the future begins to look more beautiful than the past feels painful.

Sometimes, We Need to Forgive Ourselves

There is also something else we often forget when we talk about forgiveness.

Sometimes, the person we need to forgive is ourselves.

We need to forgive ourselves for the times we stayed when we should have walked away.

For the times we trusted when we had every reason to be careful.

For the times we gave too much of ourselves because we genuinely believed that love, kindness and patience could fix everything.

We need to forgive ourselves for the decisions we made when we did not yet know what we know today.

Because we are constantly becoming.

The person you were yesterday did not have the understanding you have today.

Maybe they were simply trying to find their way.

Maybe they were loving with the heart they had.

Maybe they were doing the best they could with the knowledge, courage and strength they had at that moment.

Be gentle with that version of yourself.

You don't have to dislike who you were to become who you are meant to be.

Growth is not about looking back at our old selves with shame.

It is about looking back with tenderness and saying,

"I understand now. And I am proud of how far we have come."

Forgiveness Does Not Mean Returning

And forgiving someone does not mean that you have to bring them back into your life.

You can forgive and still have boundaries.

You can forgive and still choose distance.

You can forgive someone and never speak to them again.

Because forgiveness is not an invitation to return to what hurt you.

Sometimes, it is simply permission to walk forward without bitterness.

You can wish someone well from a distance.

You can be grateful for what a person once meant to you and still accept that their chapter in your life has ended.

Not every ending needs to become a tragedy.

Some endings are simply the heart turning the page.

And perhaps that is one of the most beautiful things about life.

We are always being given another page.

Another morning.

Another chance.

Another beginning.

Another opportunity to become softer without becoming weaker, wiser without becoming bitter, and stronger without allowing the world to take away the kindness that lives within us.

Making Room for Something New

So, if there is something your heart has been holding for a long time, perhaps you don't have to force yourself to forget it.

Perhaps you can simply begin by telling yourself,

"I don't want to carry this forever."

And maybe that is enough for today.

Tomorrow, you may let go a little more.

And the day after that, perhaps a little more.

Until one day, you look back and realise that the thing you once carried so carefully is no longer sitting inside your heart.

You have made room.

Room for laughter.

Room for peace.

Room for new people.

Room for new dreams.

Room for the little joys you once overlooked because you were too busy holding on to something that had already passed.

And maybe that is what forgiveness really gives us.

Not a different past.

But a lighter present.

Not the power to change what happened.

But the freedom to decide what happens next.

Because life is far too beautiful to spend all of it standing at the door of yesterday.

There is sunlight outside.

There are new roads waiting.

There are beautiful things still unfolding.

And your heart deserves to be there for them.

Choose Light

So forgive, when you are ready.

  • Forgive others.
  • Forgive yourself.
  • Forgive the moments that did not go the way you hoped.

And then, gently, lovingly, let your heart choose something new.

Choose peace.

Choose gratitude.

Choose hope.

Choose laughter.

Choose the beautiful possibility that tomorrow can hold something your yesterday never could.

Choose light.

And if you ever wonder whether letting go means losing a part of yourself, remember this:

Sometimes, letting go is not losing what we once held.
Sometimes, it is finally finding the part of ourselves that got lost while we were holding on.

And perhaps, after all the hurt, all the questions, all the waiting and all the learning, the heart does not need a perfect ending.

It only needs a little courage to open itself to the next beautiful beginning.

The heart finally believing that there is still so much more to live for.

“For every heart that feels deeply, loves wholeheartedly, and is learning to choose light, one beautiful day at a time”.

— Adhira
